# HOUSE HELP Privacy Policy

Effective: 17 September 2026

HOUSE HELP processes account details, service addresses, booking records, provider verification documents, payment references, support messages and—only during an assigned active service—provider location. Location sharing starts with provider consent and stops after completion or cancellation. Payment card data is handled by the payment gateway and is not stored by HOUSE HELP.

Data is used to operate bookings, verify providers, prevent fraud, process payments, provide support and meet legal obligations. Access is limited by role. Sensitive documents use private encrypted storage. Retention varies by legal and operational purpose; users may request access, correction or deletion where applicable.

Service providers include cloud hosting, storage, payment, maps, messaging, analytics and support vendors acting under contractual controls. Contact: vijaydalvi7332@gmail.com.

This launch draft requires review by qualified Indian counsel, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act and applicable consumer/payment rules.
